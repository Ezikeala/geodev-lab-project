# My project brief

## The questions 
Which woredas in Ethiopia have settlements located more than 5 km by road from the nearest health facility?

## Why it matters. 
The health planners could use the answer to identify areas with poor physical access to health services. The results could help them prioritize locations for new health facilities, improve service planning, and identify communities that face long travel distances to existing facilities.

## The data I need. 
- Ethiopian woreda boundaries: Polygon boundaries with woreda names and administrative codes.
- Health facilities: Point locations and facility types such as hospitals, health centers, and health posts.
- Road network: Road centerlines covering Ethiopia, preferably with road classifications.
- Settlement locations: Villages, towns, and other populated places.
- Population data: Population distribution or population counts for estimating the number of people affected.

## The data Source
- Woreda boundaries – Humanitarian Data- https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-eth 
- Health facilities – Humanitarian Data– https://data.humdata.org/dataset/ethiopian-health-facilities 
- Road network – OpenStreetMap via Geofabrik – https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/ethiopia.html 
- Settlement locations – OpenStreetMap – https://www.openstreetmap.org/ 
- Population data – WorldPop – https://www.worldpop.org/

## What I would build. 
I would build an interactive Health Accessibility Map and Woreda Dashboard showing health facilities, roads, settlements, and areas located more than 4 km by road from the nearest health facility. The dashboard would allow a health officer to select a woreda and see the number and percentage of settlements, and optionally the population, that are beyond the 5-km accessibility threshold.
